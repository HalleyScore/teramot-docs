Releases from the newest one's last 30 days show one by one; older ones fold into a block per month, whose fixes and internal changes stay collapsed since they are the bulk of a month. (dict "notes" $notes)) }}{{ end -}} {{- end -}} {{- end -}} {{- $releases = sort $releases "date" "desc" -}} {{- $categories := slice "breaking" "ux" "agent" "performance" "other" "security" "fix" "internal" -}}
